# Particle World Website Identity

## Selected direction
First approved visual concept: Quiet Research. AMI Labs-inspired white editorial layout, generous negative space, restrained violet and research-style annotations. Existing single-page static architecture is retained.

## Tokens
- Paper: #fefefd
- Ink: #1d1d23
- Secondary text: #626273
- Official violet: #6b5bd6
- Hairlines: #e1e1e8
- Content width: 1200px maximum, responsive gutters

## Typography
- Chinese: Noto Serif SC (思源宋体), regular, for an academic editorial voice.
- English: Source Serif 4, regular, optical sizing, for a complementary research-publication character.
- Technical annotations, language controls and email: IBM Plex Mono.
- Locally hosted WOFF2 subsets; fallback Songti SC / Georgia / system monospace.
- Original supplied purple dimensional mark remains unmodified. Beside it, a black text wordmark adapts the brand to the approved white layout; the original lockup asset remains intact.

## Content and behavior
- Complete Chinese copy supplied by the user, with corresponding English translations.
- Four sections: what we are building, company introduction, vision, join us.
- Chinese is the initial default; language preference persists locally.
- Language switch updates document language, title, description and accessible labels.
- Intro links navigate to relevant sections; email opens the visitor's email client.
- Existing contact address: info@particleworld.ai. No invented jobs, addresses, partnerships or metrics.
- Responsive layout; visible keyboard focus; reduced-motion preference honored.
- All Chinese content remains available when JavaScript is disabled.

## Scope
Local development and preview only. Do not deploy without a new explicit request.
